Innovative Solutions

Finding answers in the 5th Dimension.

Reality is experienced in four dimensions: three spatial and one temporal. All contemporary research happens within them. Quantum mechanics argues for a fifth, where the laws of physical reality give way and answers emerge in the unconscious realm.

Two realms

The domain of doing, and the realm of being.

3rd Dimension

The domain of consciousness

Rigid and predictable, transactional by nature. It lives in the dimension of doing and obeys cause and effect: advertise more to attract more sales, run a campaign to lift website hits.

5th Dimension

A realm of being

Far more fluid and flexible. In quantum mechanics it encompasses parallel universes, where past, present and future collapse into a single instant. Game theory shows how it can be applied in business.

The infinite game

Finite players fight price wars and chase last year's targets. The infinite player manifests non-linear solutions, supporting itself by going with the flow of life. This ensures longevity, and aligns perfectly with branding.

Accessing the 5th Dimension

Divination: the Tarot.

The Tarot, like dream interpretation, visualisation or meditation, is a way of tapping into the unconscious, which Jung described as holding a collective, universal component. It seeks answers through a process called mirroring. Suppose an agency has pitched for your business and you turn to the cards for guidance.

Card I

Eight of Swords

The agency is outside its comfort zone, unsure how to communicate and likely to struggle delivering on its promise, the blindfolded figure on a slippery road who could cut herself if not careful.

Card II

Five of Pentacles, reversed

The company has just emerged from financial difficulty and is in self-recovery, but healing will take time. Outcome: be cautious, and reconsider this supplier.

Datavision uses a range of divination card systems in its assessment of the 5th Dimension, applied across qualitative research, branding and personal life alike.

Numerology

The name connects you to the Source.

By analysing the content and context of any name, business or personal, you connect to a universal Source that shapes your unconscious engagement in life. Does a name have the capacity to generate wealth? To communicate and connect? For a business, how does it align to its actual operations? Changing a spelling, adding a letter or rescheduling a launch date can shift that engagement going forward.

Arrow #1

Innovation & resourcefulness

The chart reveals Datavision functions from a strong disposition of innovation and resourcefulness.

Arrows #2 & #3

Reflective & resourceful

Reflective and understanding toward customers and staff, with the cunning ability to merge resources to achieve material goals.

Using the Arrows of Pythagoras, the line of actualisation also shows where a name lacks capacity. Datavision performs both personal and business numerological name-chart analysis, helping people and companies reach their full potential.
